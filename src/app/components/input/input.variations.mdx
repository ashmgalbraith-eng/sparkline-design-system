## States

Inputs support five states: default, focus, error, success, and disabled. Error and success states surface contextual feedback text below the field.

<Canvas of={InputStories.States} />

## With icons

Icons can be placed at the leading or trailing position inside the field. Use them to reinforce the input's purpose (e.g. a search icon) or provide inline actions.
