# Card

Cards are surface containers that group related content and actions. They accept any content via `ng-content` and come in three visual styles.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Playground} />

## Usage guidelines

<Callout>
Choose the card variant based on background context: elevated cards work on flat backgrounds, outlined cards suit layouts where shadow would create noise, and filled cards work on light canvases that need subtle grouping.
</Callout>
