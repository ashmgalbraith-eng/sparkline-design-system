## Types

Three variants establish visual hierarchy on a page. Use filled for the primary call to action, outlined for secondary actions, and text for tertiary or low-emphasis actions.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Types} />

## Sizes

Two sizes are available. Use large (default) in most contexts. Small is suited for dense layouts or toolbars.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Sizes} />

## States

Buttons respond to all standard interaction states. The disabled state prevents interaction and removes the element from focus order.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.States} />

## With icons

Icons can appear on either side of the label, or the button can be icon-only. Use icons to reinforce the action — not as decoration.
